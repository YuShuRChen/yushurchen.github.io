Hi, my name is Yu-Shu Chen. I also go by Richard.

I am a Master's student in the Electrical and Computer Engineering Department at UC San Diego, where I also got my Bachelor's degree in Computer Engineering with a minor in Cognitive Science.

I am broadly interested in improving AI models' abilities and interpretability by drawing inspiration from our understanding of the human brain. Specifically, I am currently working on enhancing AI's reasoning capabilities by exploring how smaller models can function as different types of memory within larger systems. My long-term goal is to advance machine learning toward artificial general intelligence and apply it to different domains in our lives.

You can find more details about my projects on my [Projects](/projects/) page. Feel free to reach out to me if you'd like to discuss ideas, collaborations, or anything else.
